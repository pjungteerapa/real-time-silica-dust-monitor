Interested in Real-Time Silica Dust Monitoring?

This project aims to support safer decision-making in dust-generating environments by displaying real-time dust sensor readings in a clear and accessible dashboard.

The system is designed for monitoring airborne dust levels across multiple locations, helping users compare sensor readings, identify unsafe conditions, and support future improvements in workplace safety.
